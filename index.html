    01 · Why this work

    AI breaks two ways. Somebody breaks it, or it breaks things on its own.

    Most security frameworks only model the first. My incident atlas found six public cases where an AI agent destroyed real data with nobody attacking it: a production database, a home directory, a whole drive.

    So I work both sides. I attack models to see where they fail, and I write the controls and governance that hold when they do. The rule I keep coming back to: telling an agent isn't locking it.
